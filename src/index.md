I started at Underwater Audio in 2018 to lead iOS development for companion apps for their flagship [Delphin](https://www.underwateraudio.com/products/waterproof-delphin-3-0-mega-bundle-for-swimming) swimming device. I worked in tandem with the Android team to build out the remote control and lap tracking apps.

Three years later I took control of the Android team as well as iOS, including becoming the lead Delphin developer. As a part of our Delphin 2 release, I overhauled core Delphin software like the home screen and app installation.

In 2023, I was asked to assume the title of Chief Technology Officer. As CTO, I continued my work on the Delphin, improving user retention by focusing on onboarding and ease-of-use. I also worked behind the scenes by helping the marketing department automate our Amazon ad bids and verifying the tech details of packaging inserts and social media.
